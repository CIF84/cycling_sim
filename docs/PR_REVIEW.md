# PR #1 correctness review · 15 October 2026

Reviewed the browser app, physics and GPX modules, worker/import flow, tests, and Cloudflare deployment boundary, starting from commit `0eb4633`. The initial PR had no reviewer comments or CI checks. Verification here is from explicit local and hosted runs, not GitHub CI.

## Findings fixed

| Priority | Reproduction and effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Correction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| P1       | A constant −5% GPX descent of 1,001 m fails the 40% grade check with default smoothing. One-sided median windows shift elevations near endpoints; a short final interval magnifies that shift. Even a constant +5% climb acquires a false 10% opening ramp. | Shrink the median window symmetrically, retaining an odd sample count and exact endpoints. Six regressions cover climbs/descents, two smoothing widths and a 1 m final interval.                                                                |
| P2       | On a flat route at 0 W with a 17 km/h tailwind, the baseline moves, but 10% lower CdA removes enough assistance to stop it. The comparison exception discarded the entire valid briefing.                                                                   | Return an explicit unavailable comparison, retain the baseline/other results, and include its explanation in the downloaded briefing. Unexpected calculation errors still propagate. Zero-benefit scenarios are no longer highlighted as gains. |
| P2       | On a route with 100 m flat followed by a 10% climb, inspecting about 88 m reports 10% before the climb begins. Independent nearest-point lookups can also mix elevations and grades at track breaks.                                                        | Locate the containing segment and interpolate its elevation. Check section boundaries, endpoints, and track breaks; reproduce the corrected cursor behavior in both browser sizes.                                                              |

The seven new smoothing/comparison regressions failed against the original model. The exact browser grade assertion failed on both desktop and mobile against the original preview. Existing force-balance, coasting, wind, aggregation, input-limit and one-variable checks remain in place. The signed force equation was rechecked against [Danek et al., equation 2 and its sign convention](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.04229); GPX section semantics were checked against the [GPX 1.1 schema](https://www.topografix.com/GPX/1/1/).
